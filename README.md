# India CPI Inflation Analysis 📊

## Overview

What actually drives inflation in India?

This project explores India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) using monthly data and Excel to understand how inflation changed over time, which categories contributed to it, and how major events affected consumer prices.

Instead of looking only at the headline inflation number, I broke the analysis into different perspectives to understand the story behind the data.

---

## What I Explored

### 🧺 1. CPI Basket Contribution

I grouped detailed CPI categories into broader categories and calculated their contribution using an equal-weight approach.

The goal was to understand which major areas of the CPI basket had the highest calculated contribution in the latest available month.

### 📈 2. Inflation Trends

I calculated annual Rural + Urban CPI inflation and compared year-on-year changes to understand how India's inflation rate evolved over the years.

The analysis also separates 2023 as a Year-to-Date period because the dataset ends in May 2023.

### 🍎 3. Food Inflation

Food is an important part of consumer spending, so I analyzed Food inflation over the 12 months ending May 2023.

This includes:

- Month-on-month Food inflation
- Highest and lowest monthly movements
- 12-month change in the Food Index
- Individual Food categories with the largest changes

### 🦠 4. COVID-19 Impact

I used March 2020 as the COVID-19 reference milestone and compared inflation before and after the onset period.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall CPI
- Food
- Healthcare
- Essential Services

This helped identify how inflation patterns changed around the pandemic period.

### 🛢️ 5. Imported Crude Oil & Inflation

I compared monthly movements in imported Indian Basket crude oil prices with monthly CPI category movements from 2021 to 2023.

I used Excel's `CORREL` function to identify which CPI categories moved most closely with changes in imported oil prices.

The analysis also highlights an important point:

**Correlation shows a relationship between variables, but it does not prove causation.**

## Dashboard

The final Excel dashboard brings the major findings together in one place.

It includes:

- CPI category contribution

The dashboard was designed to make the analysis easier to understand without going through every calculation sheet.
